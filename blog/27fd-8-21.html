Twenty-seventh Day - Final Day - 8/21/26

Published: August 21, 2026

For my final day I started off taking helpdesk calls. First was a tough one where a Zebra label printer was printing too far offset to the right. It was fixed by matching the left alignment with another printer. The next call was about a laptop's keyboard not working in Epic; but after having the user sign out and back into Epic, the issue was fixed. The last helpdesk issue I fixed was with a scanner not having the options the user needed like double-sided scanning. Turns out they had the wrong scanner selected which didn't have those options. Then I went on a site visit to change the location of some monitors. A couple of older monitors were moved upstairs where nice monitors weren't as neccessary but dual monitors were. It also meant A doctor got newer, much nicer monitors. I was actually a bit jealous of the setup after I got it done. It was a very good, productive last day.
