On Monday I had a challenging call right in the morning helping a non-technical user diagnose a monitor issue which was resolved eventually. I was able to explain the correct procedure to them. Another thing I did was getting guided through the process of printing badges, which is complicated by strict and obtuse licensing from the card printer software and the printer itself.
