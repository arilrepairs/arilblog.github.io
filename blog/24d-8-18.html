Twenty-fourth Day - 8/18/26

Published: August 19, 2026

First call I got was someone having an issue with their internet connection. I couldn't reach them on the network so it in fact was, and the issue was fixed just by having the user reboot the machine. Another person had an issue with a script their department uses creating an invalid 0-kb file. They ended up talking themself through the issue on the phone. Even though I didn't do anything, I still created the ticket to show something wrong had happened in the first place. Maybe something wrong happens with the script again and we have data to prove it failed in the past. I got another call from someone trying to have 1password autofill a certain form field it wasn't autofilling. That one was pretty fun to diagnose and fix as they were a fun person to talk to over the phone! The last call I got was the practice manager of one of the clinics having issues with a dirty scanner which was leaving a vertical black like in the resulting scan. After having them clean it one more time, it was still dirty, so I deemed it necessary to go on-site and fix it myself. Turns out they had been cleaning the glass with a clorox wipe and not with isoproyl alcohol which leaves a ton a residue and often doesn't pick up debris. After cleaning it with an alcohol prep pad, it was fixed. I know now to ask what they're cleaning their stuff with. Chances are it's not the right thing.
