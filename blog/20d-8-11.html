Published: August 12, 2026

I was shown the procedure for interdepartment delivery which uses a courier service, and I went on a site visit to install a couple webcams. On one laptop, Teams had crapped the bed and wouldn't work with the built-in webcam even though Windows itself could use it just fine. It was a simple fix of going into Windows settings >gt; Apps >gt; Installed apps >gt; Microsoft Teams >gt; Advanced options >gt; Terminate then Repair.
